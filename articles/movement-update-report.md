# Movement Update: Kashmir Protest and Crackdown Report

## Overview
In early 2026, the Joint Awami Action Committee (JAAC) announced a peaceful shutdown and protest campaign around the demand to secure 12 assembly seats. The movement was meant to be nonviolent, focused on closing shops and holding peaceful marches, especially toward Rawalakot city.

## Timeline of the Crackdown

### June 5: Internet Shutdown and Early Crackdown
- On June 5, the government shut down the internet across all of Azad Jammu and Kashmir.
- That same night, security forces carried out a major operation in Poonch Division, particularly in Rawalakot.
- Dozens of residents were killed in the crackdown.

### June 9: Peaceful Movement and Massacre
- JAAC continued its peaceful campaign, including closing shops and marching toward Rawalakot.
- On June 9, rangers opened fire on civilians in the streets.
- They reportedly fired into the District Headquarters (DHQ) where injured people were sheltering.
- Multiple doctors were killed while trying to help victims.
- Video evidence of this massacre is available in `evidences/mirpur-division/9-june`.

### July 27: Election Boycott and Evening Attack
- On July 27, elections were held despite widespread public refusal to participate.
- More than 70% of the local population did not attend the election.
- In the evening, security forces attacked citizens returning to their homes.
- The attackers used live fire, killing over 20 people in Rawalakot and injuring hundreds.

### July 28: Mirpur Prayer Gathering Fired Upon
- On July 28, people gathered in Mirpur for prayers and solidarity for the martyrs.
- Security forces again opened fire during the gathering.
- At least 12 people were killed.
- Since then, forces have become more aggressive and continue to crack down on protestors in multiple areas.

## Evidence and Documentation
- Video and photographic evidence of the violence have been collected.
- The available files are stored in the `evidences/` directory, including the June 9 massacre evidence under `evidences/mirpur-division/9-june`.

## Legal Violations by Pakistan
Based on the events described, the following serious violations occurred:

1. Internet shutdown across AJ&K, blocking communication and freedom of expression.
2. Refusal to return dead bodies to families, violating basic humanitarian and legal rights.
3. Use of live fire against peaceful civilians, including during prayers and marches.

## Conclusion
This report documents a series of violent actions against a peaceful movement in Azad Jammu and Kashmir. The shutdown of communication, repeated shootings, and refusal to release bodies are clear signs of escalating conflict and potential human rights violations. The evidence available in the repository should be preserved and shared to support international awareness and legal accountability.
